Who We Are

Saygınlar Transport was founded by Mert 22 on June 25, 2024. The goal of Saygınlar Transport is to foster friendship and camaraderie—primarily within TruckersMP, as well as in other games—and to ensure everyone has an enjoyable time.

What We Do

  • Regular convoys and events
  • Training and guidance for new members
  • Technical support and member support
  • Truckers Book tracking and company management
  • Partnerships with other VTCs (see the Partnership page)

Our Purpose for Establishment

  • To create a warm, loving, and respectful family environment through friendly chats and sincere conversations.
  • To foster a warm, loving, and respectful family environment through sincere chats and friendly connections, not only in ETS2 but across all our games.

How to Join

You can join our team by applying through TruckersMP and completing a brief registration process on Discord. We are happy to answer any questions about the application and joining process right there on Discord.
