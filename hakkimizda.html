Who We Are

Saygınlar Transport is a Turkish virtual trucking company (VTC) for players who play Euro Truck Simulator 2 on TruckersMP. Our goal is to enjoy driving together in a reliable, friendly and organised environment.

What We Do

  • Regular convoys and events
  • Training and guidance for new members
  • Technical support and member support
  • Truckers Book tracking and company management
  • Partnerships with other VTCs (see the Partnership page)

How to Join

To join, you can apply through TruckersMP and come to our publicly open Discord server to meet the team. We answer questions about the application and joining process on Discord.
